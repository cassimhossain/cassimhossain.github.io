About

Engineer. Builder. Teacher.

I sit at an intersection most people never reach. I have a Mechanical Engineering degree from NUST, I'm completing my MSc in Robotics and AI, and I've spent two years proving that complex AI knowledge can be made accessible — at scale, across every kind of institution Pakistan has.

That combination means I approach AI not as an abstraction but as a system that has to work reliably in the real world — with real constraints, real failure modes, and real humans on the other end.

I build practical tools with that same discipline. Not demos. Not MVPs that break in front of users. Things that actually work — and I write about how they work, clearly enough for anyone to follow.

Pakistan has one of the youngest populations on earth. The engineers being built today will define the next decade. That is what the education side of my work is about.
